# Independent review of the Career Discovery Prototype

Offline reproductions used fictional records and temporary storage. Line numbers refer to `work/reviews/pre-review-source`, and the aggregate observations refer to the published reports and replay.

## Confirmed findings, in priority order

1. **P1 — Mandatory conditions can disappear.** `career_discovery/analysis.py:246–294`. A B1 German profile with two matching duties reaches **actionable** for `Requirements: German B2, C1 preferred.` The entire line becomes preferred. `German B2 is required.` below `Additional information` is discarded. Parse mixed intent in bounded clauses and retain explicit personal requirements from neutral sections. Both ads must exclude B1 and allow B2 to satisfy the language requirement, with C1 separately preferred. Keep benefits and company boilerplate out of eligibility.

2. **P1 — Scoped tenure can be credited from unrelated experience.** `career_discovery/eligibility.py:21–23,143–159`. With `relevant: 36, sql: 0`, `Minimum 2 years of experience with SQL.` is met while `Minimum 2 years of SQL experience.` conflicts. Recognise equivalent placement before and after “experience”. Both must conflict with zero SQL months and stay unknown if SQL tenure is missing. A generic tenure requirement can still use `relevant`.

3. **P1 — Third-party and hypothetical acts become direct evidence.** `career_discovery/analysis.py:196–229`, `matching.py:95–114`. `Supported colleagues who built dashboards and analyzed data` receives two direct task tags. `Reviewed QA results to decide whether to run experiments` receives experiment credit. Withhold direct tags when the action belongs to somebody else or describes a possible future act. Retain valid candidate statements such as `Built dashboards with colleagues` and `Ran experiments to test QA changes`.

4. **P1 — Availability checks can verify a soft failure and invalidate a manual check.** `career_discovery/sources.py:241–260`, `sourcing.py:53–68`, `storage.py:218–225`. An exact ATS URL returning `200` with only `<h1>Employer home</h1>` is marked verified. `search --verify` also replaces an already manually verified ATS-source custom careers link with unverified, making zero requests. A same-version feed refresh resets an explicit manual closure to listed. Require a positive job-specific page or application signal, omit unsupported automatic targets before counting the cap, and preserve explicit closure for the unchanged listing until positive reopening evidence. Test each with a stub and isolated storage.

5. **P2 — Bullet formatting changes task membership, and repetition inflates fit.** `career_discovery/analysis.py:261–268`, `matching.py:123–173,216–229`. `Analyze sales data and build dashboards` plus four unmatched duties vanishes from task results. The same two verbs on separate bullets produce a verify-first result. Conversely, `Analyze sales data` and `Analyze customer data` reuse one evidence line and task tag for strong fit. Count clearly independent action/object pairs consistently across formatting and require distinct supported capabilities for strong fit. Keep low-coverage rows review-only.

6. **P2 — Some unresolved requirements have no review exit, while seniority goes unchecked.** `career_discovery/eligibility.py:176–183`, `matching.py:133–185`, `sourcing.py:72–86`. Even with `Strong communication skills.` listed verbatim in `verified_requirements`, a `Your profile` clause remains unknown. `Lead Analyst` with two generic matched duties is actionable for an early-career profile. Add a documented, explicit review resolution for ambiguous ad clauses. Reuse seniority signals to hold an early-career mismatch for review when ownership or scope is unknown, without rejecting based on title alone. Test both ranking modes on those cases.

7. **P2 — The blind pilot drops useful ranking diagnostics.** `career_discovery/pilot.py:13–41`, `evaluation.py:123–154`, `docs/pilot.md:7–9,34–40`. The pack prioritises actionable lists and samples only task verify-first. The curated replay has zero actionable for either mode, zero task verify-first and six baseline verify-first. Those six only have a chance of entering a random sample. The evaluator scores no review lane or missed positive jobs, and blank ratings on displayed jobs yield precision and link rate of zero. Pool both methods’ top review results symmetrically, keep lane membership in the private key, and report lane-specific coverage and missed relevant cases on fully labelled corpora. Show label coverage and incomplete status alongside any all-shown rates. Exercise a zero-actionable, baseline-review-only synthetic deck and a partially filled rating file.
