# Changelog

## 0.3.0 (20 November 2026)

- My applications: tick two or three saved jobs and compare them side by side (status, pay, deadline, posting age,
  supported duties, duties not in your CV yet, open mandatory requirements, conflicts, languages).
- README screenshots of the main pages, made with fictional data.
- Fixes: items you add yourself no longer say "From your CV: Added by you"; the first heading in a box no longer leaves a
  gap; the duty search no longer has a hard-coded special case for one German phrase.

## 0.2.0 (20 November 2026)

- Local web app for non-technical users: double-click starters for macOS, Windows and Linux, English and German interface.
- Guided steps: CV upload and review, personal details, Find jobs, Your matches; plus My applications and Your data.
- Matches explain themselves: the ad's duty next to the CV line that backs it, conflicts, language levels, pay, deadline,
  posting age and help with wording an application. Reasons appear in German in the German interface.
- Your matches: a "What you are looking for" bar with a Change button, and a search over title, company, place and duties
  in English or German.
- Find jobs: an optional search of three more Berlin employers (Trading 212, bunch, Upvest) next to the standard 12
  boards; CSV import with an opt-in to keep job descriptions.
- Analyser version 16 and catalogue version 4: better duty recognition, three new task kinds, onboarding sessions for new
  customers.
- Diagnostics: three independent held-out sets, a CV-line scorer and a profile yield script.

## 0.1.0 (17 November 2026)

- Reviewed command-line prototype (git tag `baseline-2026-11-17`): profile import and review, evidence-based and title
  ranking, public Greenhouse and Ashby boards, availability checks, feedback, export and deletion.
